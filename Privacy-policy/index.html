Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digifello ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the TutorDesk Android app (package name com.digifello.tutordesk). By using the app, you agree to this policy. For more information about us and our apps, visit digifello.dev.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up we collect your email address and password. Sign-in and password recovery are handled by Firebase Authentication, so your password is never stored by us in readable form. We may also store your name or profile details if you add them.

Information you enter about your tuition business

  • Batches: batch name, class time, class days, monthly fee and number of students.
  • Students: student name, optional phone number, and which batch the student belongs to.
  • Payments: fee amounts expected and paid, payment status, and totals earned. We do not collect bank, card or UPI details, and payments are not processed inside the app. You only record them.

AI question generator content

If you use the question generator, we process:

  • The study material you upload (PDF files or images).
  • Your requirements (number of questions, language, syllabus).
  • Your Gemini API key.
  • The paper details you enter (institution name, class, time allowed, total marks) and the papers you choose to save.

Information stored on your device

Your Gemini API key is saved in encrypted storage on your phone. The app also keeps simple preferences, such as whether you have finished the welcome screens.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than files you choose to upload), microphone or camera data, or advertising identifiers.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show your batches, students, schedule, fees and earnings.
  • To generate question papers and answer keys when you ask for them.
  • To send necessary account emails, such as verification and password reset.
  • To keep the app secure, fix problems and improve it.

We use your data only to run the app for you. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

3. Services we use

To run TutorDesk we rely on trusted providers. They process data only as needed to provide their service.

ServicePurposeData involved
Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) Sign-in, email verification, password reset, and storing your batches, students and payment records Email address, account identifiers, batches, students, payments
Our cloud server (hosted on Render) Handles question generation and PDF creation Uploaded files and your API key while a request is processed, generated papers
MongoDB Atlas Stores papers you save, and temporary text extracted from your uploads while you generate questions Saved papers, temporary text chunks and their numeric embeddings
Google Gemini API Creates questions from your study material Uploaded files, your requirements, your API key

Use of Google services is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Gemini API usage on your key is billed or limited under your own Google account terms.

4. AI question generator and uploaded files

When you generate questions, the files you upload are sent securely to our server, which passes them to Google's Gemini API using the API key you provided. Generated questions are returned to your phone. For larger documents, we temporarily store text extracted from your upload, split into small pieces together with numeric embeddings, so that questions can be regenerated without uploading again. This temporary data is deleted when you finish or leave the generator, and in any case is removed automatically within 6 hours. Papers are stored permanently only if you tap save.

Please do not upload material that contains sensitive personal information about students, and only upload content you have the right to use.

5. Student data and your responsibilities

As a tutor, you decide what student information to add. You are responsible for entering only information you have the right to store, and for removing a student's record if the student or their parent or guardian asks. TutorDesk is meant to be used by tutors (adults). It is not directed at children, and children should not create accounts.

6. Data sharing

We share data only in these cases:

  • With the service providers listed above, to run the app.
  • If required by law, court order or a valid legal request.
  • To protect the rights, safety or security of our users or the app.

We never sell your personal data.

7. Data security

All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption. Your data is accessible only through your signed-in account, and your API key is stored in encrypted storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details private.

8. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account information, batches, students, payment records and saved papers, except where we must keep something to meet a legal obligation. Backups are cleared within a reasonable period.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your batches, students and payments inside the app.
  • Deletion of records: you can delete batches, students and saved papers within the app.
  • Account deletion: see section 10.
  • API key: you can remove your Gemini API key from the question generator at any time. You can also revoke it in Google AI Studio.

10. Delete your account and data

To delete your account and all data linked to it, email us at digifello.official@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "TutorDesk account deletion request". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. If you only want certain data removed (for example a student's record or saved papers), tell us what to delete in the same email.

11. Children's privacy

TutorDesk is intended for adults who teach. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Continuing to use TutorDesk after a change means you accept the updated policy.
